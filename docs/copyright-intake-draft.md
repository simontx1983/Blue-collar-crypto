# Copyright reporting draft

Owner and counsel review is required before publishing the page copy below. The owner must complete the designated-agent filing. Confirm the service provider, designated agent, monitored intake channel and active U.S. Copyright Office directory entry before publication.

## Public page copy for review

### Copyright concerns

Upload images you created or have permission to share. If you believe material on Blue Collar Crypto infringes copyright, open **Report a copyright concern** on the material or use the general legal contact in our Terms. Include the Blue Collar Crypto post or profile URL and identify the specific image. For a comment, include its identifier if shown in the draft email. The report link opens an email draft for you to review and send.

### Copyright removal notices

If you own the copyright or are authorized to act for its owner, send a written notice to the verified designated agent. Include your physical or electronic signature, the copyrighted work or a representative list, a description of the material and where to find it, and contact details. State that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorized by the owner, its agent or the law. State that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act for the owner.

Designated agent contact details will be added after verification. Please avoid putting contact details or legal notices in public comments or ordinary in-app reports.

### Counter-notices

If your material was removed or made inaccessible due to copyright and you believe this happened by mistake or misidentification, you may send a written counter-notice to the verified designated agent. Identify the removed material and where it appeared. Include your signature, name, address and telephone number, and state under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification. State that you consent to the federal district court for the district where you live or, if you live outside the United States, for a district in which the service provider may be found. State that you will accept service of process from the original complainant or their agent.

A valid counter-notice is shared with the original complainant. Access is ordinarily restored within 10 to 14 business days after receipt unless the complainant gives notice of a filed court action seeking to restrain the activity. We may terminate repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances.

## Staff process template

1. Route general copyright concerns to the approved intake mailbox and formal notices to the designated agent. Assign a restricted case record, record the date received and use the post or profile URL with its media location. A general moderation flag may help locate material. Formal notices, signatures and contact details belong in the restricted record.
2. Review promptly for completeness, authorization, the specific material and the requested action. Follow up on correctable omissions. Escalate unclear ownership, claimed exceptions, abuse, litigation or legal-process questions to counsel. Handle emergency or legally required retention separately from the visibility decision.
3. When warranted, remove or disable access to the specific material promptly. Check the applicable surface, including cached or derivative media when appropriate. Record the action and promptly notify the uploader of the affected material, the reason and the counter-notice process. Use existing moderators' authorized removal controls where they cover the material. Escalate media without a suitable removal control instead of deleting unrelated content.
4. Check counter-notice completeness, including jurisdiction and service-of-process statements. For a valid counter-notice, promptly forward a copy to the original complainant and communicate the planned restoration. Track 10 to 14 business days from receipt, and document any timely notice of a filed court action. Have counsel review uncertain timing or conflicting legal requirements.
5. Document restoration or the reason it was withheld. Track substantiated events and their outcomes under the owner-approved repeat-infringer process. Correct records for withdrawn, reversed or misidentified notices and use proportionate escalation under the approved policy. Counsel should review termination criteria and any appeals process.
6. Restrict access to notices, counter-notices, contact details, decisions, timestamps and communications. Store case records outside public Git repositories and ordinary moderation notes. Confirm the retention period, deletion permissions, legal-hold rules and accountable operator before activation.
