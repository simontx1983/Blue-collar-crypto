Artist Statement · Written by @welder

A few words from the studio

I weld for a living — forty hours a week in a shop in eastern Ohio. The work on this page started as warm-up drawings of the seams I was laying that day. Arc light on steel, cooling metal, the bead you leave behind when you do it right.

Editions are small, prices are fair, every piece is signed from the shop wallet. If you hold one, you held something I made with my hands. I respond to every message from a collector, usually on my lunch break.

— J. Welder, studio
